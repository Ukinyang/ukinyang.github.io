# Ukeme Inyang

Personal website of Ukeme Inyang, marine ecologist and environmental data analyst based in Bremen, Germany.

**Live site:** https://ukinyang.github.io

## About the site

The site brings together my research, data analysis and experience in one place:

- **Research:** my MSc thesis at the University of Bremen on the mesozooplankton community of the Lagos Lagoon, and my MSc project at the University of Lagos on bacterial communities in the Lagos and Badagry lagoons
- **Species found:** stereomicroscope photos of mesozooplankton from the Lagos Lagoon survey
- **Data analysis:** figures and R code from the thesis analysis, including abundance, diversity, cluster analysis and NMDS
- **Experience and education:** research roles, studies, volunteering and training
- **Contact:** email and LinkedIn

## Repository structure

```
index.html    the website (a single HTML page)
figs/         thesis figures used in the data analysis section
species/      stereomicroscope photos used in the species section
```

The site is a static page hosted with GitHub Pages. Any change pushed to the `main` branch goes live within a few minutes.

## Use of images and content

All photographs, figures and text on this site are my own work. Please do not copy, download or reuse them without my permission. The raw research data are not included in this repository.
